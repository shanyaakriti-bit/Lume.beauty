How to book

Six simple steps

Bookings are made by enquiry only, not through Instagram messages, so every client gets the same clear process.

  1. Check availabilitySee which dates are open on our availability calendar.
  2. Send an enquiryFill in the enquiry form with your date, look and details. We reply within 48 hours.
  3. Free 15-minute consultationIf your date is available, we'll send you a link to book a short call to plan your look.
  4. Sign and secureYou'll receive the booking agreement to sign online, plus an invoice for 30%. Your date is held once both are done.
  5. Second paymentPay 20% at least 48 hours before your appointment.
  6. Final paymentPay the remaining 50% once your makeup is complete.
