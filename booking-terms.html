Booking terms

Booking Agreement

Clear terms keep every appointment calm and on time. You'll sign these online before your date is held.

1. Services

  • Lumé provides makeup services only. We do not offer hair styling and do not bring pins, clips or hair accessories.
  • Your look is agreed at your consultation using reference photos. On the day, we can make small adjustments within your appointment time.
  • Each person's look is decided between that person and the artist.

2. Payments

  • 30% booking fee is due within 48 hours of receiving your invoice and secures your date. It is non-refundable.
  • 20% is due at least 48 hours before your appointment. If it is not received, your booking may be cancelled and the booking fee kept.
  • 50% balance is due when your makeup is complete, before you leave the studio or before the artist leaves your location.
  • For group bookings, the person who makes the booking is responsible for payment for the whole group.

3. Changes and cancellations

  • You can reschedule once with at least 7 days' notice. Your payments move to the new date, subject to availability.
  • Cancelling more than 48 hours before your appointment: the 30% booking fee is kept.
  • Cancelling within 48 hours, or not attending: all payments made (30% and 20%) are kept.
  • If Lumé has to cancel, you can choose a new date or a full refund of everything you've paid.

4. Being ready on time

  • Please be ready at your start time, showered, with clean, moisturised skin and no makeup.
  • If you arrive late, or aren't ready at your start time, your appointment still finishes at the booked time, which may mean a simpler look. Your full price still applies.
  • If you are more than 30 minutes late, your appointment may be treated as a late cancellation.

5. On-location bookings

  • Please confirm your full, correct address 24 hours before your appointment. Time lost from a wrong address comes out of your appointment.
  • We need a quiet space with a table, a chair, a power point and good light, and a hand to carry our kit inside.
  • Travel and early-start fees are listed on our Prices page and included in your invoice.
  • Our artist may end an appointment if the environment is unsafe or abusive. Fees for services already completed remain payable.

6. Your skin and health

  • Tell us about any allergies, sensitivities or skin conditions before your appointment. A patch test is available on request.
  • We may decline to apply makeup over broken, irritated or infected skin.

7. Your look on the day

  • We check your look with you before finishing. Please tell us during your appointment if you'd like anything adjusted, so we can fix it then.
  • These terms don't limit your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act.

8. Photos

  • We may photograph your makeup for our portfolio and social media. Just let us know if you'd prefer we didn't.

9. Agreement

Your booking details (name, date and start time, location, number of people and looks, total price and payment amounts) are filled into your personal agreement, which you sign online. By signing, you confirm you have read and agree to these Booking Terms.
