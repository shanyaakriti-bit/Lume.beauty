Services & looks

Find your look

Know exactly what each look includes, how long it takes and how to arrive. Times are approximate.

The Soft Glam

About 60–75 min

Skin prep, a lightweight buildable base matched to your skin tone, concealer, soft contour and blush, softly blended eyes, defined brows, mascara and a flattering nude or soft lip, finished with setting spray.

Best for: daytime events, birthdays, engagements, and anyone who loves a fresh, natural finish.

The Full Glam

About 75–90 min

Full skin prep, a long-wear base with colour correcting, sculpted contour and highlight, defined or statement eyes with liner, sculpted brows and a polished lip, set for all-day wear.

Best for: evening events, weddings, cultural functions and photos.

False lashes add-on

Adds about 10 min

Lash strip trimmed and fitted to your eye shape.

Best for: adding definition to any look.

Bridal & large events

Quoted after consultation

A custom plan for the bride or a group, including an optional trial.

Best for: weddings and functions with 3 or more people.

Good to know

Before you book

Makeup only. We don't offer hair styling, so please arrange your hair separately.

Makeup for events looks fuller than everyday makeup. Photos and lighting soften makeup, so a camera-ready soft glam will look more defined in person. We agree your look with reference photos before the day, so there are no surprises.

Your skin comes first. Products are matched to your skin tone and type, using a mix of high-end and drugstore favourites chosen for how they perform.

How to prepare

On the day

  • Arrive (or be ready, for on-location) showered, with clean, moisturised skin and no makeup
  • Have your hair dry and done, or plan it around your makeup time
  • Bring or send 2 to 3 reference photos of looks you love
  • Tell us about any allergies, skin sensitivities or skin conditions when you enquire
